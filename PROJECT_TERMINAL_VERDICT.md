# PROJECT TERMINAL VERDICT

Date: 2026-08-23

## Answer

No. The current evidence does not show that this project is going to change the world.

The terminal machine disposition is:

```text
TESTED_ARCHITECTURE_CLAIM: FAIL
WORLD_IMPACT: NOT_ESTABLISHED
RESEARCH_HARNESS_INTEGRITY: PASS
STOP_DECISION: STOP_TESTED_ARCHITECTURE_BRANCH
```

The project found a useful narrow engineering principle—keep complete evidence outside the model and compile small auditable task packets—but it did not establish a general memory architecture, a transferable incident-reasoning product, a company, or societal impact.

## Claim under test

A learned memory system, or its simpler transparent state-compiler successor, can preserve complete external evidence and select a bounded working set that materially and reliably outperforms equally informed conventional methods on unseen long-history tasks.

## Check

The claim was progressively attacked with frozen mechanisms, strong nulls, a recency-confound repair, natural-language extraction, real incident telemetry, conventional retrieval, safe full-context controls, a disjoint exact-cause benchmark, hidden scorer labels, fresh stability samples, content-addressed receipts, and exact replay.

| Gate | Terminal result | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| DMC-04B | `DMC_04B_COMBINED_LEARNED_MEMORY_ADVANCES` | Frozen learned retention and retrieval compose on the bounded synthetic benchmark. |
| DMC-05A | `DMC_05A_CONVENTIONAL_RETRIEVAL_DOMINATES` | No architecture advantage on the original generator. DMC and Recent-16 retained the same records in every one of 592 cases per seed. |
| DMC-05R | `DMC_05R_TRANSPARENT_INDEX_DOMINATES` | DMC survives repaired non-recency tails, but an equally informed transparent 16-record index matches it with less machinery. The learned-retention branch stops. |

## Verdict — FAIL

MCO-05 is the decisive branch stop. Its integrity checks pass, so this is a valid negative result rather than a broken experiment.

The frozen compiler retains the true cause in only 23/28 code-cause cases (82.14%, below 90%). The model over its packet is correct on 12/36 cases (33.33%; Wilson 95% interval 20.21%–49.67%), 0/12 adversarial cases, and 1/8 no-code cases. Its 5.56-point raw lead over hybrid RAG is only two cases and cannot compensate for the failed preregistered quality gates.

The limitation is reproducible: semantic decision stability is 12/12, live and replay prediction seals match exactly, all 216 live/replay reasoning receipts validate, and all scorer-isolation, provenance, citation, capacity, and recomputation checks pass.

## Assumption register

- MCO-04 and MCO-05 use public benchmarks, not organic prospective production incidents.
- RootCauseBench contains fictional reconstructions and fault injections. Public documentation describes scenario mechanisms; isolation was enforced in execution, but the experimenter was not blind to the benchmark family.
- MCO-04 establishes root-service localization only. The frozen reasoner identifies the exact fault class in 0/63 scientific cases.
- MCO-05 covers 36 scenarios with one frozen local 8B reasoner. It falsifies this implementation and product claim, not every imaginable compiler or model.
- All final work is self-verified locally. No independent lab, operator, customer, or prospective deployment has replicated it.
- MCO-03 extraction latency is not used as clean evidence because an unrelated Ollama workload contaminated that timing run.
- DMC historical training reconstructs 10,880 optimizer steps. Wall time, energy, and dollar cost remain `TRAINING_COST_UNKNOWN`; zero online optimization never means free learned memory.
- Local billed inference cost of $0 does not mean zero compute cost. Pretrained-model training cost is unknown and nonzero.

## Credit assignment

- Learned DMC receives credit only for a bounded synthetic composition mechanism. It receives no architecture, product, or impact credit.
- The transparent compiler receives credit for exact structured-state mechanics and MCO-04 service localization. MCO-05 shows that this credit does not transfer to reliable exact causal attribution.
- The frozen reasoner receives no independent MCO-04 credit because direct compilation was already perfect. In MCO-05 it cannot repair missing candidates and performs poorly on abstention and adversarial cases.
- Conventional hybrid retrieval remains a serious control. It does not formally dominate MCO-05, but it trails by only two cases while the packet fails its absolute gates.
- The verification harness receives engineering credit for detecting leakage, accounting errors, unnecessary learned components, transfer failure, and replay mismatches. Its commercial value has not been tested.

## Verification gap

No further experiment under the same architecture claim is justified. Reopening it would require a genuinely new, preregistered mechanism and an independently administered hidden workload—not threshold tuning or redesign against these 36 labels.

A world-impact claim would additionally require prospective organic incidents, independent replication, operator utility, production reliability, security, latency and cost economics, customer adoption, and evidence of durable effects. None exists here.

## Stop/continue

- **Stop** learned retention and the tested general AI-memory thesis.
- **Stop** the frozen transparent state-compiler product branch after MCO-05.
- **Do not** run DMC-05B or tune against terminal scientific failures.
- **Preserve** the compiler and all receipts as narrow research evidence, not as a product claim.
- **Continue only as a new thesis** if pursuing the verification/evaluation harness or a domain-specific deterministic automation product. Those paths require their own market and production gates and inherit no world-impact credit from DMC/MCO.

## Maturity status

Terminal negative for the tested transferable architecture; mature local evidence for several narrow benchmark mechanisms; early and unvalidated as a product; no established company or world impact.

The honest lasting result is:

> Store evidence cheaply, reason selectively, and make every selection auditable—but do not mistake that sound systems principle for a proven new cognitive architecture.
