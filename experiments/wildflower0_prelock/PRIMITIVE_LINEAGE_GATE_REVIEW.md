## What the historical lineage already established inside its tested toy scopes

### PRIMITIVE-0: machine-native interchange has a representation frontier

The reference implementation ended `TRADEOFF_FRONTIER`.

- minimal triples/quadruples were insufficient for parts of the semantic task family;
- several richer candidates passed most tasks but failed contradiction handling;
- the symbolic-plus-latent candidate passed T1-T8;
- deterministic replay was preserved;
- raw JSON byte size was explicitly not treated as an encoding verdict.

The C1 stable-reference repair then showed two smaller repaired candidate-C variants passing all T1-T8 and ended:

`BOTH_REPAIRS_SEMANTICALLY_SUFFICIENT_COMPARE_SYSTEM_COST`

This matters for WILDFLOWER because the historical result does **not** say “use the biggest packet.” It says semantic sufficiency can depend on a small missing reference/identity capability and must be compared at system cost.

### PRIMITIVE-1: packet-native inference itself was demonstrated

Terminal verdict:

`PACKET_NATIVE_INFERENCE_DEMONSTRATED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

The authoritative trajectory was numeric-only, deterministic replay passed, and all eight frozen inference tasks passed. This is stronger than demonstrating packet transport alone.

For WILDFLOWER, natural language therefore does not need to be the internal reasoning substrate merely to obtain compositional toy inference.

### PRIMITIVE-2: learned packet-native inference was demonstrated

Terminal verdict:

`LEARNED_PACKET_INFERENCE_DEMONSTRATED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

Three preregistered seeds reached perfect local frozen-task metrics in that experiment.

This eliminates another weak objection to the WILDFLOWER direction: packet-native reasoning need not remain entirely hand-coded.

### PRIMITIVE-2A / 2B: local competence did not imply recursive stability

2A was `PARTIAL`: two of three seeds passed, while the all-seed gate required all three.

2B then produced the more important result:

`RECURSIVE_INSTABILITY_DEMONSTRATED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

The learned reasoner changed the distribution of its own future inputs. A model that looked strong under local finite evaluation generated downstream states in which its errors amplified.

This is the same failure class WILDFLOWER independently encountered with open-loop world-model rollout.

The shared lesson is now a hard design rule:

> **One-step/local accuracy cannot authorize recursive use. Recursive use requires its own closed-loop or open-loop stability exam.**

### PRIMITIVE-2C: generic confidence/voting admission control was insufficient

Terminal verdict:

`NO_GENERIC_CONTROL_SUCCEEDS`

Majority, unanimity, swap-consistency, and related generic admission controls failed to provide a robust recursive safety mechanism.

For WILDFLOWER this is a warning against turning the successful Nursery authority score into a universal confidence scalar. The Nursery authority result is narrow: observed prediction innovation controlled authority in a specific dynamical task. It is not a generic truth score.

### PRIMITIVE-2D: reversible dependency was sufficient once errors were detected

Terminal verdict:

`REVERSIBLE_DEPENDENCY_SUFFICIENT_FOR_DETECTED_ERRORS_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

A multi-support justification DAG could revoke a detected bad derivation and downstream unsupported consequences while preserving conclusions that retained independent support.

This produces a second WILDFLOWER design rule:

> **Generated internal conclusions should be revocable state with explicit support provenance, not irreversible facts merely because a model emitted them.**

### PRIMITIVE-2E / 2F: derivation cleanliness is not truth; independent world evidence matters

2E ended `VERIFICATION_SIGNAL_INSUFFICIENT`: generic internal signals were not adequate truth/challenge signals.

2F ended:

`INDEPENDENT_WITNESS_VERIFICATION_DEMONSTRATED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

The central lesson is direct: a clean derivation history does not make a conclusion true when independent world evidence contradicts it.

For WILDFLOWER, a machine-native thought can be internally coherent and still require sensor/world verification before it obtains high epistemic authority.

### PRIMITIVE-2G / 2H / 2I / 2K: verification grew into active experiment design

The lineage then demonstrated, within progressively frozen toy scopes:

- 2G: `ACTIVE_WITNESS_ACQUISITION_DEMONSTRATED_WITHIN_TESTED_SCOPE`

This is much closer to developmental learning than simply retrieving more stored text.

### PRIMITIVE-2S / 2S.1: identifiability, not implementation effort, became the frontier

Both ended `PARTIAL`.

The post-freeze 2S.1 diagnosis found that the hidden repeated inner loop changed integer magnitudes, but the frozen observable interface exposed only the coarse state `(reg0>0, reg1>0)`. Under that readout, the repeated-loop system and a normalized simpler graph were behaviorally equivalent.

Therefore the benchmark could not identify topology necessity from its outputs.

This is a particularly important WILDFLOWER warning:

> **A failed architecture-discovery test can be an observability/identifiability failure. Before concluding that an internal mechanism is unnecessary, the evaluation interface must make the mechanism's consequences distinguishable.**

This rule should be carried into every future “did the machine discover X?” experiment.

## Convergence with the fresh WILDFLOWER result

The fresh Nursery result and the historical PRIMITIVE lineage converge on two different kinds of authority.

### 1. Predictive authority

Question:

> When may a learned world-model proposal override a transparent/persistent null?

Nursery-1 supplied one narrow working example: recent numeric prediction innovation can gate the learned correction and restore one-step safety while retaining multi-horizon benefit.

### 2. Epistemic authority

Question:

> When may a machine-native derived conclusion become or remain committed as usable knowledge?

The PRIMITIVE lineage says internal generation alone is not enough. Commitment should depend on reversible support provenance and, where needed, independent world evidence.

These should not be collapsed into one scalar confidence.

## Candidate architectural synthesis: dual authority

The next WILDFLOWER architecture candidate should therefore separate at least:

```text
WORLD / SENSOR STREAM
        |
        v
PERCEPTUAL / DYNAMICAL STATE
        |
        +---------------------------+
        |                           |
        v                           v
TRANSPARENT NULL               LEARNED PROPOSAL
        |                           |
        +--------> PREDICTIVE AUTHORITY GATE
                                  |
                                  v
                           PROVISIONAL PREDICTION
                                  |
                                  v
                        MACHINE-NATIVE DERIVATION
                                  |
                         SUPPORT / DEPENDENCY DAG
                                  |
                    +-------------+-------------+
                    |                           |
                    v                           v
             INTERNAL SUPPORT            WORLD WITNESS / TEST
                    |                           |
                    +--------> EPISTEMIC AUTHORITY GATE
                                  |
                     +------------+------------+
                     |                         |
                 COMMITTED                 PROVISIONAL /
                 KNOWLEDGE                 REVOKED / TEST
```

The two gates answer different questions and must have different evidence contracts.

A learned prediction may have enough predictive authority to drive a simulation step while still lacking enough epistemic authority to become durable semantic memory.

Likewise, an internally supported proposition can remain provisional until an independent sensor/world witness resolves it.

## What is reusable now

Reusable as design principles or hostile controls:

1. numeric/machine-native inference can be tested independently of language rendering;
2. stable-reference identity is a first-class requirement;
3. recursive stability must be tested separately from local accuracy;
4. no generic confidence/voting score receives universal authority by assumption;
5. derived state should carry reversible support provenance;
6. independent world evidence outranks self-confirming derivation cleanliness;
7. uncertainty may justify active evidence acquisition and intervention;
8. model/representation failure should permit bounded escape rather than forced best fit;
9. simplicity and system cost remain controls against unnecessary representational growth;
10. identifiability must be tested before a negative topology/mechanism verdict.
