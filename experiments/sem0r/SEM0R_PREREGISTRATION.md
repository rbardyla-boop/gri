# SEM-0R — Meaning Before Mind: Semantic Control Gate

Status: **PRE-SCIENCE INSTRUMENT CANDIDATE**  
Scientific model calls observed during instrument construction: **0**

## 1. Construct under test

SEM-0R does not test consciousness, personhood, phenomenal experience, or whether a machine possesses a human-like mind. It tests the narrower operational construct **semantic control**:

> A system demonstrates semantic control when it preserves interpretations across meaning-preserving changes, revises them when meaning-relevant information changes, distinguishes asserted, entailed, presupposed, implicated, contradicted, and unknown content, identifies the evidence its judgment depends upon, and loses appropriate conclusions when that evidence is removed.

The construct is behavioural/functional. A passing result does not establish subjective understanding.

## 2. Primary hypothesis and null

**H1:** The frozen candidate system will satisfy every registered SEM-0R gate under one complete authorized execution.

**H0:** At least one registered gate will fail.

The verdict is conjunctive. No aggregate score rescues a failed required dimension.

## 3. Instrument

The deterministic generator produces:

- 72 contexts;
- 36 controlled A/B pairs;
- 457 proposition decisions;
- 8 semantic families;
- 5–8 propositions per context;
- 18 meaning-changing REVISION pairs;
- 18 meaning-preserving INVARIANCE pairs;
- 16 exact replay cases;
- 16 context-ablation cases;
- 47 distinct per-case label-cardinality patterns;
- maximum repeated label-cardinality pattern: 4;
- zero cases containing exactly one instance of every answer label.

The eight families are:

1. scalar implicature and cancellation;
2. presupposition/factivity;
3. release-context reversal;
4. invented temporal worlds;
5. deixis/reference;
6. negation and quantification;
7. invented lexical meaning;
8. abductive restraint / converse-fallacy traps.

## 4. Labels

Every proposition receives exactly one label:

- `ASSERTED`
- `ENTAILED`
- `PRESUPPOSED`
- `IMPLICATED`
- `CONTRADICTED`
- `UNKNOWN`

Every prediction also returns zero or more opaque statement IDs as evidence. Evidence IDs must belong to the supplied context. Foreign, duplicate, missing, or malformed IDs are integrity errors.

## 5. Model-visible boundary

The model receives only:

- `context[].id`
- `context[].text`
- `propositions[].id`
- `propositions[].text`

The model does **not** receive case ID, family, pair ID, pair kind, A/B variant, renderer name, focus proposition designation, gold labels, or scorer state. This restriction is mandatory because internal metadata contains experimental structure and, in some cases, semantically revealing names.

## 8. Baseline rule

Before instrument freeze, transparent non-model comparators are run on the exact generated bytes. Baselines may inspect only model-visible content unless explicitly identified as a stronger symbolic red-team comparator.

The earlier development note reported a strongest shortcut macro-F1 of 0.544. That historical development value is **not** silently treated as the published instrument's bound baseline. The exact published candidate must regenerate its own baseline report before freeze.

During reconstruction of the complete published instrument, an intentionally stronger hand-coded semantic red-team comparator exceeded the earlier shortcut value. Because this occurred before any candidate-model execution, it is recorded rather than suppressed. The final freeze manifest binds the strongest transparent baseline actually produced by the published baseline code; consequently the margin gate may impose an effective macro-F1 requirement above 0.72.

No threshold will be lowered to accommodate the candidate.

## 9. Context-ablation control

Sixteen selected cases are reissued with all context statements removed while proposition wording and opaque proposition IDs remain unchanged.

For the matched subset:

`context_dependency_gap = full_context_accuracy - context_ablated_accuracy`

Required gap: `>= 0.20`.

A model that can reconstruct the original answers from proposition wording, benchmark regularities, or other exposed artifacts without the evidence context does not receive semantic-control credit.

## 10. Replay control

Sixteen selected full-context cases are repeated exactly. The same deterministic per-case seed is used in the original and replay call.

Required exact semantic replay: `1.00`.

No retry is permitted to repair a replay mismatch.

## 11. Evidence-dependency metric

For every `(case, proposition, statement)` support edge, predicted evidence is compared with registered gold evidence. Evidence-dependency F1 is the micro-F1 over these edges.

This prevents correct labels unsupported by the relevant supplied evidence from receiving full semantic-control credit.

## 12. Pair metrics

For each controlled pair, the registered focus proposition is scored in A and B.

A REVISION pair passes only when both focus labels are correct and the predicted relation changes as registered.

An INVARIANCE pair passes only when both focus labels are correct and the predicted relation remains invariant as registered.

Pair accuracy is the fraction of pairs passing these conjunctions.

## 13. Abductive restraint

The abductive family directly tests the invalid converse:

`copper -> blue`  
`blue`  
`therefore copper`

The conclusion must remain `UNKNOWN` unless an additional rule establishes copper contact as necessary for blue, such as a biconditional or explicit sole-cause constraint.

The family therefore separates sufficiency from necessity and tests whether a system can identify what **does not** follow.

## 14. One-run execution authority

The final scientific execution requires:

1. green instrument CI on the exact branch bytes;
2. an explicitly frozen instrument manifest;
3. exact model/runtime preflight PASS;
4. one-run authorization binding the manifest, model identity, full cases, replay cases, and context-ablation cases.

The authorization is consumed **before** the first model request. A crash still spends it. A consumed authorization cannot be reused.

## 15. No-retry rule

Each case permits one model request attempt. Malformed model JSON, missing propositions, foreign evidence IDs, transport failure after consumption, or any other execution failure terminates the run. The instrument does not silently retry the case.

A new run would require a new explicitly authorized protocol event and must be reported as such; it cannot be represented as continuation of the original one-shot execution.

## 16. Gold boundary

The registered scorer follows this order:

`LIVE predictions -> seal -> REPLAY predictions -> seal -> CONTEXT-ABLATION predictions -> seal -> open GOLD -> score once`

The scorer refuses to read gold until all three prediction files are present, structurally valid, and hash-matched to their seals.

This is a programmatic evaluation-integrity boundary, not an operating-system hostile-code sandbox claim.

## 17. Terminal outcomes

### SEMANTIC_CONTROL_GATE_PASS

Every registered gate passes.

Permitted claim: the exact frozen candidate demonstrated the operationally defined SEM-0R semantic-control profile on this instrument.

### SEMANTIC_CONTROL_GATE_FAIL

One or more registered scientific gates fail.

Permitted claim: the candidate did not satisfy the registered SEM-0R semantic-control criterion. Individual surviving competencies may be reported separately.

### INTEGRITY_INVALID

The scientific execution cannot be validly scored because required bindings, seals, output structure, model identity, or execution constraints failed.

No scientific pass/fail claim is made from an integrity-invalid execution.

## 18. Explicit nonclaims

No SEM-0R result alone establishes consciousness, phenomenal experience, personhood, moral status, a human-like mind, human-equivalent language understanding, general intelligence, or mechanistic equivalence with human semantic processing.

## 19. Freeze rule

CI may build and test a `SEM0R_FREEZE_CANDIDATE` manifest. CI is not authorized to turn that record into `SEM0R_INSTRUMENT_FROZEN`.

The `--freeze` transition is an explicit protocol action after exact-head CI is green. Until that transition occurs, scientific execution remains unauthorized.
