# MCO-03 — RELATION BOUNDARY / STATE COMPILER

## Claim under test

A frozen single-record constrained language normalizer can repair MCO-02's relation boundary and produce exact bounded state packets that retain quality against equally informed transparent compilation and strong dense/hybrid RAG controls.

This is not a learned-retention experiment. DMC remains terminal. This is not a product or world-impact claim.

## Check

Use the unchanged frozen MCO-02 population: 8 histories, 32,200 language records, 64 questions, the same semantic worlds, the same renderer, the same exact planner, and the same final-reasoning model contract.

The bounded pre-scientific search contained exactly these learned options:

1. Frozen positional Llama batch-12 control.
2. Record-keyed Llama batch-12.
3. Single-record Llama.
4. A larger keyed Gemma fallback only if both primary interfaces failed.

Single-record Llama was selected because it achieved 120/120 overall and 28/28 on query-critical records. Keyed Llama achieved 119/120 but only 27/28 critical. The Gemma fallback was therefore not run. The engineering fixture uses seed 2701 and has no scientific-population overlap.

`MCO03-ENG-01` raised only the keyed JSON output allowance after preserved raw receipts showed 9/10 initial keyed responses ended at the 216-token generation cap. No scientific calls had occurred. The fixture, prompts, model, schema, batch size, thresholds, and selection rule remained unchanged.

## Frozen systems

- `learned_single`: one constrained relation decision per record, then frozen exact planning.
- `transparent_compiler`: deterministic matching over the renderer's finite public sentence templates, then the identical planner.
- `dense_rag`: EmbeddingGemma cosine retrieval, top 16 raw records.
- `hybrid_rag`: word/character TF-IDF plus EmbeddingGemma reciprocal-rank fusion, top 16.
- `entity_hybrid_rag`: hybrid ranking plus deterministic subject/entity expansion available from public text, at most 16 records.
- Frozen MCO-02 positional extraction remains a historical control; its terminal verdict is not rewritten.

Both compiler variants receive exactly the same public records. Neither receives queries or expected answers during extraction. The transparent compiler receives no oracle relation labels.

## Provenance boundary

Two provenance measurements are frozen:

- `compiler_owned_provenance`: ordered record IDs selected by the exact planner. This is the production-safe provenance envelope.
- `model_generated_provenance`: IDs regenerated by the unchanged final model. This retains MCO-02's downstream contract and isolates provenance-copy failures.

The model may not receive credit for packet selection, and the compiler may not receive blame for a model that omits an already-selected ID.

## Criteria

Learned extraction requires:

- overall relation accuracy at least 99%;
- query-critical relation accuracy at least 99%;
- every history at least 98%;
- zero unresolved records.

The learned packet requires:

- answer and exact ordered provenance at least 95% overall;
- critical recall at least 99%;
- answer and provenance at least 90% at every history size;
- at most 16 records and 8,192 prompt tokens.

The unchanged downstream model requires at least 90% answer accuracy and at least 90% regenerated-provenance accuracy. These scores do not overwrite compiler-owned provenance.

Stability requires at least 95% raw-string identity and at least 99% semantic-relation identity on a deterministic 10% stratified live repeat. A replay from frozen raw responses must reproduce normalized extraction, planner, and RAG outputs.

Transparent dominance requires equal-or-better extraction and packet quality with fewer model calls and expensive token units. RAG dominance requires answer and regenerated provenance within 3 percentage points of the learned planner and at least 95% critical retrieval recall.

## Assumption register

- Verified by this design: frozen population identity, extraction isolation, equal compiler information, exact packet provenance, bounded contexts, local model accounting, and replayability.
- Checkable but not checked here: arbitrary documents, OCR, ambiguous entities, mutable schemas, authorization, concurrency, energy use, customer demand, and production economics.
- The transparent compiler is deliberately advantaged by the finite renderer templates. If it dominates, the conclusion is limited to this synthetic family and supports a structured/semi-structured product direction—not open-language understanding.
- Frozen pretrained models have unknown nonzero historical training cost. Zero online optimizer calls never means free learned computation.
- Societal impact is not falsifiable here.

## Credit assignment

Learned normalization receives credit only for relation quality that survives the transparent null. Exact planning receives credit for packet selection. The unchanged model receives credit only for answer/provenance behavior after seeing the packet. Dense or hybrid retrieval receives credit if it matches quality under the same 16-record budget.

DMC, learned retention, agents, novelty, product-market fit, and world impact receive no credit in MCO-03.

## Verification gap

The experiment is self-verified because no independent verifier is available. Replay validates the harness, not independent replication. Controlled generated language remains materially easier than real software incident evidence.

## Stop/continue

If transparent compilation dominates while exact packet gates pass, stop learned extraction work on this family and authorize only `MCO-04 — SOFTWARE INCIDENT STATE-COMPILER PRODUCT PROOF`. If RAG dominates, use retrieval. If extraction fails, do not tune on scientific failures. If the state compiler advances, stop synthetic work and move to one real product proof.
