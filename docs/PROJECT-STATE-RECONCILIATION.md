## Largest remaining unresolved question

The largest remaining unresolved question in the overall program is the parent
question itself:

> What is the smallest executable digital primitive that can retain and
> transform useful state over recurrence, under matched resource constraints,
> without merely matching a simpler finite-state or stateless control?

GRI-05 answered the narrower geometric comparison negatively: the baseline
mean primary score was `.775`, SO(4) was `.325`, and all four preregistered
advancement gates failed. RRI-01 further diagnosed relation erasure in the
baseline's long-depth dynamics. Those results constrain the parent question but
do not establish a minimum state size, transition cost, or universal lower
bound.

The unresolved question is not:

```text
whether minimality has been proved
whether any broad cognitive claim follows
```

None of those claims is established by the repository.
